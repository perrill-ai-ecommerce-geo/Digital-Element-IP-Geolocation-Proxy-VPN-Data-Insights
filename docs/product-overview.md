# Product overview (public descriptions)

This repo references two Digital Element product lines commonly associated with ecommerce decisioning:

## NetAcuity (IP geolocation + intelligence)
High-level capability: IP address location resolution (country/region/city/postal/ZIP) plus surrounding IP/network metadata for analytics, personalization, and risk context.

## Nodify (Proxy/VPN intelligence)
High-level capability: Detect proxy/VPN connections (including residential proxies) and provide contextual information about anonymization services and traffic risk.

> For definitive product packaging, fields, and SLAs, use the vendor documentation/contract.
