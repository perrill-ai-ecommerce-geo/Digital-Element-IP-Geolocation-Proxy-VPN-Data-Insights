# 10 Must-Have IP Data Fields for B2B Attribution

For B2B marketers, the biggest challenge is "The Dark Funnel"—high-value prospects visiting your site anonymously. Digital Element’s enterprise data allows you to de-anonymize this traffic at the corporate level. 

Here are the 10 most critical fields for building a robust B2B attribution engine.

---

### 1. Company Name
- **The Value:** Direct identification of the organization visiting your site.
- **Use Case:** Instantly alert sales reps when a "Target Account" is browsing high-intent pages like pricing or demos.

### 2. NAICS / SIC Codes
- **The Value:** Industry classification codes (e.g., 541511 for Software Publishers).
- **Use Case:** Segment your traffic by industry to see which sectors (e.g., Healthcare vs. Finance) are showing the most interest in your product.

### 3. Domain Name
- **The Value:** The official web domain associated with the IP (e.g., `company.com`).
- **Use Case:** Map visitor sessions directly to your CRM records (Salesforce/HubSpot) based on the domain.

### 4. ASN (Autonomous System Number)
- **The Value:** The unique identifier for the network owner.
- **Use Case:** Helps distinguish between a major corporate network (e.g., Apple's private network) and a generic ISP.

### 5. Home/Biz Classification
- **The Value:** Identifies if the connection is a "Home" or "Business" line.
- **Use Case:** Filter out residential traffic to focus your marketing analytics purely on corporate-intent visits.

### 6. Company Headquarters Location
- **The Value:** The physical HQ location of the visiting company.
- **Use Case:** Route leads to the correct regional sales team based on the company's head office, even if the visitor is at a branch office.

### 7. Employee Range / Company Size
- **The Value:** Estimated size of the organization.
- **Use Case:** Prioritize "Enterprise" accounts (>5,000 employees) over "SMB" accounts in your automated outreach.

### 8. Connection Speed & Type
- **The Value:** Differentiates between enterprise-grade fiber and mobile/broadband.
- **Use Case:** High-speed corporate fiber is a strong signal that the user is physically inside a corporate office rather than working from a cafe.
